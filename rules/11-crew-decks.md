# 11. The Crew Decks (p.34–35)

Six asymmetric decks. Complexity is the designers' rating out of 10.

| Captain     | Faction   | Complexity | Cards | Status card?              | Recommended first game |
| ----------- | --------- | ---------- | ----- | ------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Picard**  | Starfleet | **1** / 10 | 24    | No                        | ✅                     |

## 11.1 Picard — Starfleet (complexity 1)

A diplomat first and foremost: making friends with `ALLY` cards pays off through his captain
ability, his endgame scoring and his first mission alike. Research-leaning crew: Doctor Crusher and Will Riker (via Farpoint Station) give
an early Research rise. **Data** is flexible and self-expanding — an engine capable of mastering
any single Specialty, especially with the **Daystrom Institute**. **Worf** leads Away Teams into
the Neutral Zone; the **Phasing Cloak** lets him out-Romulan the Romulans.

**Ships:** U.S.S. Enterprise-D, U.S.S. Bozeman, Type 7 Shuttlecraft.
**Endgame:** 1 VP per `ALLY` you own.
**Episodes (TNG):** Encounter at Farpoint; Measure of a Man; Darmok; The Outcast; Cause and
Effect; The Pegasus.

## 11.2 Shran — Andorian (complexity 2)

Commander of the Andorian Imperial Guard's _Kumari_. Equips any `WEAPON` he needs to defend
Andoria. Lieutenants **Tarah** (guns) and **Talas** (politics) both deploy Away Teams around
Andoria or into the Neutral Zone. Hidden talent: **bartering** — amass Dilithium (to sustain
**Imperial Pride**), then pivot late to Latinum and treasure. Watch the contested moon of
**Weytahn**.

**Ships:** Andorian Cruiser, Kumari.
**Note:** Shran himself carries the `IMPERIAL` trait.
**Episodes (ENT):** The Andorian Incident; Shadows of P'Jem; Cease Fire; Proving Grounds;
Babel One / United / The Aenar.

## 11.3 Koloth — Klingon (complexity 3)

Dahar Master of the Klingon Defense Force. A fleet of **D7 battle cruisers** arrives fast at any
contested Location; the **Experimental Cloaking Device** deploys Away Teams undetected. The spy
**Arne Darvin** raids enemy supplies; enemies go to the mines of **Rura Penthe** for a steady
Dilithium stream. His biggest achievements require accumulating **Glory** — usually by picking
fights. **Good Day to Die**; **Kor** and **Kang** aid a properly Klingon player.

**Ships:** I.K.S. Devisor, I.K.S. Gr'oth, I.K.S. Klothos.
**Episodes (TOS unless noted):** Errand of Mercy; The Trouble with Tribbles; Day of the Dove;
A Private Little War; More Tribbles, More Troubles (TAS); Blood Oath (DS9); The Sword of Kahless
(DS9); Once More Unto the Breach (DS9).

## 11.4 Sisko — Starfleet (complexity 4)

Commands Deep Space 9; deep in **Influence** politics. **Odo** manages the criminal element,
**O'Brien** makes any old ship fly, **Kira** defends Bajor. The **Wormhole** and the Prophets
give foresight; the **Orbs** are the ultimate wisdom payoff. Later: the **U.S.S. Defiant** (the
only cloaked Starfleet vessel), **Dr. Bashir** (an Augment!) and **Garak**. For war, Starfleet
and **Worf** reinforce from **Starbase 375**.

**Ships:** Deep Space 9, U.S.S. Defiant, U.S.S. Rio Grande.
**Episodes (DS9):** The Emissary; Past Prologue; Necessary Evil; The Search I & II; House of
Quark; Call to Arms; Behind the Lines; Image in the Sand / Shadows and Symbols.

## 11.5 Sela — Romulan (complexity 5)

Shadowy commander of the Romulan fleet. `SHADY` tactics plus **General Movar**'s underworld
connections give wide options. Three **D'Deridex**-class warbirds start in her deck, **each with
its own cloaking device** — naval superiority across the Neutral Zone. Mid-game fork: politics
at gunpoint via **Proconsul Neral**, **Commander Donatra** and the **Valdore**; or a pact with
the devil — the **Remans**, **Praetor Shinzon** and the **Scimitar**.

**Ships:** I.R.W. Goraxus, I.R.W. Khazara, I.R.W. Terix, I.R.W. Valdore, Scimitar. _(5 — the most.)_
**Episodes (TNG unless noted):** The Enemy; The Defector; Data's Day; Redemption I & II;
Timescape; Unification I & II; The Search Part I & II (DS9); Star Trek: Nemesis.

## 11.6 Burnham — Starfleet (complexity 7) — **special rules**

Post-Burn Dilithium scarcity. Dilithium is more precious than Glory. **Theta Zeta** is the
mystery at the heart of her deck. The Federation is fractured: **Earth**, **Ni'Var** and the
**U.S.S. Federation** HQ come online late. Crew: **Detmer + Owosekun** (away missions),
**Adira Tal + Tilly** (deck optimisation), **Dr. Culber** (fixes people), **Stamets** (spore
drive). The **U.S.S. Discovery-A** is free to show up at any Location; **Booker** is on the lookout for a `CREATURE`
needing transport.

**Ships:** Book's Ship, U.S.S. Discovery-A, U.S.S. Federation.
**Status card:** **Inert Dilithium**.
**Episodes (DIS):** That Hope Is You I & II; People of Earth; Forget Me Not; Die Trying;
Unification III; Su'Kal; There is a Tide; Kobayashi Maru.

### 11.6.1 The Inert Dilithium rules

These are **active while the Inert Dilithium Status card is in play**. Theta Zeta **logs** the
Status card; once logged, **all of these rules switch off**.

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Hand size is 6, not 5.** Draw 6 in player setup step 13 and refill to 6 each Clean-up.                                                                                                                             |
| 2   | **Clean-up:** instead of placing Glory on the Market, **remove 1 Glory from the current Stardate card** and **place 2 Dilithium from the supply** on one Market card. Whoever gains that card gains the 2 Dilithium. |
| 3   | **Any Dilithium you _gain_ via an operation goes onto the Inert Dilithium card**, not your pool. **Exception: Dilithium gained from the Market (tokens on a gained card) goes to your pool normally.**               |
| 4   | **You cannot spend Glory as if it were Dilithium.** (The normal 1 GLO = 2 DIL substitution is disabled. Glory-for-Latinum still works.)                                                                              |
| 5   | **You cannot spend Dilithium from the Status card**, and **you do not own it** for the purposes of other effects.                                                                                                    |
| 6   | **`recrystallize`**: shift **1 Dilithium** off the Status card into your **personal pool**. The ability appears on three cards: **Reno**, **Tilly**, **Stamets**.                                                    |
| 7   | **If your opponent steals Dilithium from you, give it from the Inert Dilithium card first.** (It makes no difference to them where it comes from.)                                                                   |
| 8   | **If _you_ steal Dilithium, it goes to your pool, not the Status card.** Stealing is not gaining.                                                                                                                    |
| 9   | **Cadet Training variant:** remove 1 Glory from the Stardate and place only **1** Dilithium (not 2).                                                                                                                 |

> **Implementation:** model this as a **rule-modifier layer** attached to the Status card, with
> hooks on `handSize`, `onGainResource(DIL)`, `canSubstituteGloryFor(DIL)`, `onStolen(DIL)` and
> `cleanupGloryPlacement`. Logging the Status card detaches the layer. This is the single
> strongest argument for making rule modifiers first-class in the engine rather than special-casing.
